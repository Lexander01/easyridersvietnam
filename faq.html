Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about logistics, our bikes, safety gear, and the overall experience.

Logistics & Timing

What time does the tour typically start each morning?

8:30 AM.

What time do we usually arrive at the overnight stop each evening?

Usually around 5:00 PM or 5:30 PM, or just before it gets dark.

Do you genuinely pick up from any hotel in Dalat, or just a central meeting point?

Yes, we pick up directly from any hotel or guesthouse in Dalat City.

For tours ending in Hoi An or Saigon — do you drop off at their accommodation, or at a central spot in the city?

We drop you off directly at your accommodation.

The Bikes

What brand, CC, and type are the motorbikes?

150cc Honda, Suzuki, and Winner motorbikes.

Can two people share one bike, or does each person get their own?

Both are possible. Two people can share one bike, or you can ride your own.

Is there a weight limit for passengers?

Yes, the limit is between 50kg and 120kg.

Does luggage travel on the bike with you, or is it transported separately?

Most luggage is carried on the bikes. Bulky luggage is sent separately by car courier and will be waiting at your destination.

Gear & Safety

What protective gear and rainwear do you provide?

We provide full gear, including helmets, knee protectors, rain boots, rain suits, and ponchos.

Do the guides carry a first aid kit?

Yes.

Do you have child-specific helmets and safety belts?

Yes, these are thoughtfully prepared and provided during the tour.

Money & Payment

Do you accept EUR or USD cash directly, or is Vietnamese Dong strongly preferred?

We accept credit cards as well as cash in EUR, USD, and VND.

Accommodation

What amenities are available at the overnight stops?

All accommodations include WiFi, hot water, and air conditioning.

Health & Physical Requirements

What is the minimum and maximum age for passengers?

Children from 5 years old can participate.

Are the tours suitable for people with back problems or mobility issues?

Yes.

What happens if a customer gets sick or injured mid-tour?

The guides are fully prepared to assist and will do everything necessary to help you.

The Experience

Is there a WhatsApp group during the tour, or a way for family at home to track the route?

Yes, there is a WhatsApp group during the tour.

How much English do the local villagers and guesthouse owners speak, or do the guides translate?

English proficiency varies. When communication is difficult, your guide will translate for you.

Are there sections of the route that are particularly physically demanding?

No, though we sometimes take short, easy walks or hikes to stretch our legs.

What is the coldest it gets at night — should customers pack warm layers?

The average temperature at night is around 24–25°C, and you can adjust the room temperature with the A/C.

What is the one moment or viewpoint that customers consistently say is their favourite?

This is flexible—just share your favorite types of viewpoints with the guide, and they will gladly make it happen.
